## Sunshift assets

- Art includes the procedural tiles, characters, props, and postcards in `src/art/` and the project icon in `icon.svg`.
- The music and sound effects are synthesized by `src/audio/`.
- The canvas shaders in `src/shaders/` handle the warmth grade, postcard reveal, light beams, and wind sway.
- The courtyard wishes and journal postcards live in `data/levels/` and `data/postcards.json`.
